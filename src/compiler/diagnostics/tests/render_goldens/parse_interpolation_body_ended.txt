-- A MALFORMED STRING INTERPOLATION ------------------------ test.ipe

The first `}}` ends this interpolation before its expression is complete. A `{{…}}` in a `"""` string holds one expression, and the first `}}` after the `{{` ends it.

 --> test.ipe:4:16
  |
4 |     """a {{f (x}} b"""
  |                ^^ this `}}` ends the interpolation
  |
  = help: the first `}}` ends the interpolation; balance the brackets or bind the expression to a name
