-- A MALFORMED STRING INTERPOLATION ------------------------ test.ipe

This `{{` has no `}}` after it in the string. A `{{…}}` in a `"""` string holds one expression, and the first `}}` after the `{{` ends it.

 --> test.ipe:4:10
  |
4 |     """a {{b"""
  |          ^^ this `{{` is never closed
  |
  = help: close the interpolation with `}}`, or write `\{{` for a literal `{{`
