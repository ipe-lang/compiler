The closing `"""` stands in column 7, but the opening one in column 5. A `"""` string is either one line (`"""text"""`) or a block: the opening `"""` ends its line, and the closing `"""` stands alone on its own line in the opener's column, the margin.

6 |       """
  |       ^^^ this `"""` is in column 7, not column 5

4 |     """
