# IPE-P0050: unclosed delimiter

I found an opening bracket — a `(`, `[`, or `{` — that never gets a
matching close. I read past where it should have ended and either hit
something that can't belong inside it or ran out of file, so I'm
pointing back at the opener that was left hanging.

Every opening bracket needs its partner: `(` with `)`, `[` with `]`,
`{` with `}`. They also have to nest properly — you can't close an
inner one after an outer one. The most common cause is simply
forgetting the closing bracket, or crossing them by mistake.

A parenthesised expression with no closing `)`:

```ipe ipe:error
total =
    (price + tax
```

Add the matching `)`:

```ipe
price = 10
tax = 2

total =
    (price + tax)
```

A list needs its `]`, and the brackets must nest cleanly:

```ipe
items =
    [ 1, 2, 3 ]
```
