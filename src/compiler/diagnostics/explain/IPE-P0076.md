# IPE-P0076: a triple-quoted string line left of its margin

A block `"""` string has a margin: the column of its opening `"""`, which its
closing `"""` shares. The compiler removes everything left of the margin from
every line between them, so each of those lines must start at the margin or
further right. Indentation past the margin is kept in the value.

A line that starts left of the margin is refused, because removing the margin
would cut its own text:

```ipe ipe:error
greeting =
    """
    Hello,
  world
    """
```

Indent the line to at least the margin column:

```ipe
greeting =
    """
    Hello,
    world
    """
```

A tab left of the margin is refused too: a tab is as wide as each viewer
makes it, so it cannot say how far a line is indented. Replace the tab with
spaces. A line holding only spaces may be shorter than the margin; it reads as
an empty line.
